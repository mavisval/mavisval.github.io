I worked as a researcher on the LINear to CIrcular Transition (LINCIT) project from July 2023 to June 2026. The project focuses on operational decisions in circular business models such as remanufacturing and refurbishment.

My work examined how firms can design and manage circular strategies that support economically viable and environmentally beneficial decisions. I also contributed to annual progress reporting for consortium partners and presented research findings at project meetings.

Key themes of the project include:
- Circular business models and sustainability transitions
- Remanufacturing and refurbishment operations
- Strategic decision-making in operations management
