I participated in the China Contemporary Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling in September 2017. The team developed a joint pricing and allocation model for crowdsourced task platforms and ran numerical experiments to characterize optimal policies.

This work combined mathematical modeling with computational experimentation and led to the award of Provincial First Prize in Jiangsu Province.

What the project involved:
- Design of a pricing and allocation framework for platform tasks
- Numerical experiments to analyze optimal decision rules
- Recognition through a provincial-level competition award
