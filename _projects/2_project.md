I led this project from April 2022 to November 2022 as part of a post-graduate innovation and entrepreneurship initiative. The aim was to integrate sequential game-theoretic models with prospect theory and contract theory to improve computational resource allocation in blockchain systems.

The project involved a comprehensive literature review of more than 70 papers across cooperation contracts, resource allocation in blockchain, supply chain management, and related application areas. The work combined analytical modeling with applied economic reasoning to assess how incentives and strategic behavior affect system performance.

Highlights:
- Led the project team and coordinated the research agenda
- Developed theoretical models connecting game theory and contract design
- Reviewed a broad literature base to identify relevant mechanisms and applications
