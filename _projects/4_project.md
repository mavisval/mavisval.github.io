I was a project member in an undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship project from May 2017 to June 2018. The work focused on college students' national identity and social responsibility through field surveys and quantitative analysis.

The project involved collecting questionnaire data from universities in Nanjing and applying factor analysis and linear regression in R and SPSS to develop a quantitative grading model. It also resulted in a published review paper on national identity and social responsibility of college students.

Key outcomes:
- Conducted field survey work and data collection
- Built a quantitative evaluation framework using statistical methods
- Contributed to a published review paper and received provincial priority project recognition
